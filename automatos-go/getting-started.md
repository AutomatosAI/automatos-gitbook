# Getting Started

This page takes you from installing Automatos Go to your first message to Auto. It covers hosted Studio. If you run Automatos on your own server, install the app here, then follow [Connect Your Own Server](connect-your-own-server.md).

## What you need

* An iPhone with iOS 17 or later.
* An Automatos account you already use for Studio. The app can't create one.

## Install the app

Automatos Go is being tested on TestFlight, Apple's app for test versions. It is not on the App Store yet. Testing is by invitation only. If you have no invitation, you can't install the app yet.

1. Install **TestFlight** from the App Store.
2. Open the TestFlight invitation for Automatos Go.
3. Install **Automatos Go** from TestFlight.

Updates arrive by themselves. See [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md#getting-the-latest-version) if you don't see a change.

## Sign in

The first time you open the app you see **Welcome**, with two buttons: **Sign in to Automatos** and **Connect to your own server**.

<!-- IMAGE: Welcome screen with Sign in to Automatos and Connect to your own server -->

1. Tap **Sign in to Automatos**.
2. Enter the email address of your Automatos account.
3. Choose how to sign in:
   * **Email me a code**: Automatos emails you a code. Type it in.
   * **Use my password**: type your password.
   * **Sign in with a passkey**: your phone shows its passkey sheet.
4. If your account has a second step, the app asks for it: a code by email or text, a code from your authenticator app, or one of your backup codes.

The app shows "Opening your workspace…" while it loads.

{% hint style="info" %}
There is no sign-up, and no sign-in with Apple, Google or other accounts. Use the email address you use for Studio.
{% endhint %}

## Before you talk to Auto

Owners, admins and editors see **Before you talk to Auto** once, before anything else. It names the AI providers Auto uses in your workspace and says what goes to them: your messages, answers, notes and attachments. Tap **Agree and continue**, or **Sign out** if you'd rather not. The app asks again if your workspace's providers change. Viewers are not asked, because they can't send anything. See [Privacy & AI](privacy-and-ai.md).

## Find your way around

<!-- IMAGE: Home screen showing the header, the open menu and the Needs you strip -->

| Area | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Header** | The Automatos sail and "automatos" on the left. The menu button on the right. |
| **Menu button** | Opens the menu. While something needs you, it shows a number. |
| **Menu** | Your workspace name and role at the top, then **New chat**, **Chats**, **Command Centre** (with the same number) and **Settings**. |
| **Needs you** | A strip on the home screen: "Needs you" with a count, **Command Centre →** and a card for each thing waiting on you. It is hidden when nothing needs you. |
| **The thread** | Your conversation with Auto. Home opens your newest conversation, or a new one if you have none. |
| **Message Auto** | The message box at the bottom. See [Chat](chat.md). |

Tap a card in the Needs you strip to go to it. A question opens Questions. A ticket waiting for review, stuck or failed opens that ticket. Something the phone has no screen for opens Studio in your browser.

The app has one workspace at a time. There is no workspace switcher.

## Settings

Open the menu and tap **Settings**. **Account** shows:

| Setting | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Server** | The server's name, its address and its edition: "Automatos cloud" or "Self-hosted" |
| **Workspace** | The workspace you are in |
| **Role** | Owner, Admin, Editor or Viewer |
| **App version** | The version of Automatos Go on this phone |

## Sign out

Open **Settings** and tap **Sign out**. The app goes back to Welcome. Signing out doesn't delete your account or your workspace.
