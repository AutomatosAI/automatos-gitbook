# Automatos Go

Automatos Go is the Automatos app for iPhone. Studio is where you set things up: your workspace, agents, Playbooks and themes. Automatos Go is where you talk to Auto when you are away from the desk. You ask Auto for things, answer the questions your agents raise, check the Board and open what your agents made.

In these pages, **Studio** means the Automatos web app, whether you use the hosted one or run Automatos on your own server.

<!-- IMAGE: Automatos Go home screen with the header, the Needs you strip and Auto's thread -->

## Who it is for

Automatos Go is for people who already have an Automatos workspace. There are two ways in:

* **Hosted Studio:** sign in with the Automatos account you use for Studio. See [Getting Started](getting-started.md).
* **Your own server:** pair the phone with a one-time code from Studio. See [Connect Your Own Server](connect-your-own-server.md).

The app signs in to an account you already have. It can't create an account or a workspace.

## What it does today

| Area | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Chat** | Talk to Auto. Send text, photos and files. Read formatted answers, approve Mission plans and tool requests, and go back to older chats. See [Chat](chat.md). |
| **Command Centre** | The Board, Calendar, Questions and Deliverables. Approve or send back tickets, answer questions, and pause or resume routines. See [Command Centre](command-centre.md). |
| **Needs you** | One number for what is waiting on you. It shows on the menu button, on the home screen and in the Command Centre. |
| **Your own server** | Pair the phone with an Automatos server you run, at home or on the internet. See [Connect Your Own Server](connect-your-own-server.md). |

## What each role can do

The app follows your role in the workspace. It checks your role each time it opens.

| On the phone | Owner | Admin | Editor | Viewer |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Read chats, the Board, Calendar and Deliverables | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Chat with Auto | Yes | Yes | Yes | No |
| See and answer questions | Yes | Yes | Yes | No |
| Approve or send back tickets, plans and tool requests | Yes | Yes | Yes | No |
| Pause and resume routines and scheduled tasks | Yes | Yes | Yes | No |

A viewer sees "Read only" where the message box would be, with "Ask your workspace owner for editor access to talk to Auto".

## What stays in Studio

Agents, Playbooks, themes, team members, connected apps and workspace settings are all set up in Studio. When a link in the app points to a page the phone doesn't have, it opens Studio in your browser.

## Phone and system

* iPhone with iOS 17 or later.
* Automatos Go is being tested on TestFlight, Apple's app for test versions, by invitation only. It is not on the App Store yet.
* There is no Android version yet.
