# Privacy & AI

This page says what Automatos Go sends, to whom, and what it keeps on your phone.

## The AI providers you agree to

Before owners, admins and editors talk to Auto, the app shows **Before you talk to Auto**. It names each AI provider your workspace uses: "Auto answers using AI models from" and the providers' names. It says what goes to them: what you send to Auto and your agents (messages, answers, notes and attachments), so they can reply.

<!-- IMAGE: The Before you talk to Auto sheet with the AI providers listed -->

* Tap **Agree and continue** to go on.
* If you'd rather not, tap **Sign out**. On your own server the button is **Remove this phone and forget this server**, and the app asks first.
* If your workspace's providers change, the app asks again.
* If the server doesn't list its providers yet, the sheet says so and names none.
* Your agreement is kept on this phone. The app doesn't send it anywhere.
* Viewers are not asked, because they can't send anything.

## "Auto is an AI"

A small line, "Auto is an AI", sits at the top of every conversation. When another agent answers, it names that agent. It is on the AI-providers sheet too, and VoiceOver reads it on Auto's picture. It never stops you typing.

## What the phone keeps

| What | Where it is kept |
| --- | --- |
| Your sign-in, or the key your own server gave this phone | The iPhone Keychain. It stays on this iPhone: it isn't copied to iCloud or to a new phone. |
| Your workspace, the Board, the Calendar and the Deliverables list | An encrypted copy on the phone, so the app opens quickly. It is kept for up to a day. |
| Chats and questions | Not saved on the phone. They load from the server each time. |
| Your agreement to the AI providers | Encrypted on the phone, for this server and workspace |

Signing out, or forgetting your own server, clears your sign-in and what the app kept for that server. Deleting and reinstalling the app starts clean: the first launch clears what an earlier install left in the Keychain. One thing stays: a random number that marks this phone, so if you pair it with your own server again, Studio shows the same phone instead of a new one.

Nothing is queued while you are offline: an answer or an approval either reaches the server now, or the app says it didn't.

## Photos, camera and files

* Before a photo is sent, the phone makes a fresh copy without the location and camera details stored inside it.
* The camera is asked for only when you tap **Camera**.
* The photo library and Files need no permission: the app sees only what you choose.

## Your own server

On a server you run, your chats, files and reports stay on that server. The app itself:

* checks Expo (the service that delivers the app's updates and notifications) for app updates
* sends notifications through Expo and Apple, once those arrive and only if the server's operator agrees

The note on the pairing card also mentions crash reports. The app doesn't send crash reports yet. A later update adds them, with a switch to turn them off.

## Accounts

The app signs in to an account you already have. It can't create an account, and it doesn't delete accounts or workspaces. Nothing in the app is about plans or payments.

## Report this

**Report this** lets you flag one of Auto's messages or a Deliverable that is harmful, offensive, wrong, or shares private information. You can add a short note. Your workspace owner sees the report.

## Links

When you tap a link to another site in Auto's answer, the app asks before it opens your browser. Pages the phone doesn't have open Studio in your browser.
