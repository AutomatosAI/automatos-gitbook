# Plans & Billing

Hosted Studio has three plans. You buy and manage them in **Settings → Billing**, without leaving Studio. The local edition is free and has no billing.

## Plans

**Own keys: $9 a month.** Everything in Pro, on your own Anthropic, OpenAI or OpenRouter keys. Your provider bills you for the AI.

**Pro: $29 a month.** 5 seats, 20 agents, 60 minutes of video render a month, with monthly AI credits included, so you don't need keys of your own.

**Business: $99 a month.** 25 seats, unlimited agents, 240 minutes of video render a month, and a larger monthly credit allowance. By invite: contact Automatos.

**Enterprise: coming soon.** Contact Automatos.

New accounts start with a one-time 500-credit trial. A credit is one US cent of AI usage at Automatos prices. The local edition is free and open source (Apache 2.0), on your own keys or your Claude Code / CLI subscription.

## Where to find it

| Where | What you see |
| --- | --- |
| **Settings → Billing** | Your plan, renewal date, credit balance, plans, top-ups, your card, invoices and your credit history |
| **The header** | Your credit balance, on every page |
| **Notifications** | Running low on credits, out of credits, a payment that didn't go through, a plan that ended |

Everyone in a workspace can see its plan and balance. Only the workspace's owner and admins can buy, change or cancel.
