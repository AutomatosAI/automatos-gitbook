# Command Centre

The Command Centre shows what your agents are doing and what is waiting on you. It has four tabs: **Board**, **Calendar**, **Questions** and **Deliverables**.

<!-- IMAGE: Command Centre with the head, the live word and the four tabs -->

## Open the Command Centre

* Open the menu and tap **Command Centre**, or
* tap **Command Centre →** in the Needs you strip on the home screen.

At the top, **Auto** takes you back to your chat. Beside it a word says whether the Command Centre is up to date: **Streaming live**, **Connecting** or **Offline**. Under the title, one line sums up the workspace, for example "1 agent working, 5 in queue, 6 need your eyes."

Pull down on any list to refresh it.

## What needs you

One number counts what is waiting on you: tickets waiting for review, questions, approvals, stuck tickets and failed tickets. The same number shows on the menu button, beside **Command Centre** in the menu, and in the Needs you strip on the home screen, so they always agree.

## Board

Each card on the Board is a ticket: one task for an agent. Studio calls it a task.

The Board lists your workspace's tickets. Filter by **All** or by a status. Tickets waiting on you come first: Blocked, then Review, In progress, Assigned, Inbox, Done, Failed and Cancelled.

Tap a ticket to open it. The ticket shows:

* the agent and when it was last updated
* what the ticket is about, and why it is in Review or Blocked
* **Waiting for your answer**, with **Answer in Questions**, when it waits on a question
* the **Result**, or **Why it failed**
* its **Deliverables**, with **Open**

### Approve or send back

On a ticket in Review:

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Approve** | The button says what approving does: "Approve and mark done", "Approve and publish", "Approve and start the post" or "Approve the plan". |
| **Send back** | Asks "What's wrong?". Say what to fix: the agent reads your note first and redoes the ticket from it. A note is required. |

Viewers see "Owners, admins and editors approve or send back tickets."

### Talk to Auto about a ticket

Tap **Ask Auto about** and the ticket number, for example **Ask Auto about #0419**. A new chat opens with "Discussing #0419" and the ticket's title at the top. Talk it through with Auto, then:

* **Update ticket and re-queue** opens **The agreed brief**. Tap **Update and re-queue**: the brief replaces the ticket's, and its agent redoes the ticket from it. The old brief and the last draft stay on the ticket.
* On a Mission's ticket, **Decide on the mission →** takes you to the Mission instead.
* **End the discussion** closes the bar.

Owners, admins and editors can ask Auto about a ticket and update it.

## Calendar

The Calendar shows one day at a time, with a strip of today and the next six days above it. **Always on** (marked 24/7) lists the routines that run every hour or more often. Paused items stay listed, greyed, with the word **Paused**. A deadline that has passed shows **Overdue**.

Tap an item to open it:

* **A routine or an agent's scheduled task:** **Pause this routine** or **Pause this task**, then **Resume this routine** or **Resume this task**. The sheet says what pausing does before you tap. A paused item may drop off the Calendar; Studio still lists it.
* **A deadline:** opens its ticket on the Board.
* **Anything else**, such as a Playbook or a social post: **Manage in Studio** opens it in your browser.

Nothing on the phone cancels a scheduled item. Viewers see "Owners, admins and editors pause and resume routines and scheduled tasks."

## Questions

Questions lists what your agents are asking you.

* Tap one of the offered answers to send it at once.
* Or tap **Answer in your own words instead**, type your answer and tap **Answer**.
* **Dismiss** first says what dismissing does, then offers **Keep the question** and **Dismiss**. Dismissing a question that stopped a Playbook run closes that run.

When nothing is waiting, you see "No open questions" and "Agents are deciding on their own." Owners, admins and editors see and answer questions. A viewer sees "Questions are not open to you here".

## Deliverables

Deliverables lists the files your agents made, the same list Studio shows. Each card says its type, its title, its first words and who made it when. Filter by **All** or by a type, such as Report, Image or Document: the filters list the types your Deliverables have. Tap **Load more** for older ones.

Tap a Deliverable to open it:

* **Text** shows on the page. A long file shows its first part, with "This is the first part of the file. Studio has all of it."
* **Images** show on the page.
* **PDFs, Word and Excel files** open in the phone's viewer: **Open the PDF**, **Open the document** or **Open the spreadsheet**.
* **Anything else:** **Open in Studio**.

You read and open Deliverables on the phone. You don't approve them here. **Report this** is here too once it is switched on, with a later update to Automatos.

## Approving social posts

The app has a screen for a social post waiting for approval. It shows the post as it will publish: each channel's text, the media and when it publishes. **Approve** schedules that exact version. **Request changes** sends the post back with your comment.
