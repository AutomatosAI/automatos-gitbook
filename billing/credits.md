# Credits

A **credit** is one US cent of AI usage at Automatos prices. Credits pay for AI that runs on **Automatos's AI keys**.

## What uses credits, and what doesn't

| Uses credits | Free |
| --- | --- |
| Auto and your agents, when they run on Automatos's AI keys | Anything that runs on **your own AI keys** (add them in **Settings → API Keys & Credentials**); your provider bills you directly |
| Voice: Auto's spoken answers (charged as typed answers are), and [Auto Live](../chat/voice.md#auto-live) calls, charged by the minute when the call ends | Hearing Auto's answers read aloud, and your browser or phone listening to you |
| | Free models, and your own Claude Code / CLI subscription |
| | Automatos's own background work, such as routing, memory and checking answers |

Each AI request costs what it actually used, so a short answer costs a little and a long report or app build costs more. Your credit history in **Settings → Billing** lists every charge, with the last 30 days of usage by model.

## Where your credits come from

Credits are spent in this order:

1. **Trial credits.** A one-time 500-credit trial when you create your account, once per person. Trial credits work on every plan.
2. **Monthly credits.** Pro and Business include credits every month; the Billing tab shows how many. They arrive when the month's payment goes through. Unused monthly credits don't carry over: a fresh month replaces them.
3. **Top-ups.** On Pro and Business you can buy extra credits at any time. A top-up lasts 12 months, and the oldest is spent first.

On **Own keys**, the AI runs on your own keys, so after your trial you don't need credits at all.

## Running low and running out

* When about **a fifth of your allowance** is left, you get a notification and the balance in the header turns amber.
* At **zero**, AI on Automatos's keys pauses. Chat shows a short message with two ways to continue: **top up** or **add your own AI key**.
* Your workspace keeps working, and so does anything that runs on your own keys. Nothing is lost.
* A long task never overspends: it stops before its next AI request once your credits are used up.
