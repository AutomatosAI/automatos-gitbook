# Voice

Voice lets you talk to Auto instead of typing. Hold the button, say what you want, let go, and Auto's reply is spoken back to you.

{% hint style="info" %}
**Coming in a later update.** Voice is not built yet. This page describes what is planned, so details may change.
{% endhint %}

<!-- IMAGE: The voice screen with HOLD TO TALK, the words as you speak and Auto's reply -->

## Talk to Auto

1. In a chat, tap the microphone beside the send button. The voice screen opens. Tapping it doesn't turn on the microphone.
2. Press and hold **HOLD TO TALK**. Your words show on screen as you speak.
3. Let go. Your words go to Auto as a message, in the same chat.
4. Auto's reply is spoken by your phone's own voice, and shown on screen.

To stop, tap **STOP**. Or, while you hold the button, say just "Auto, stop": that stops too, and nothing is sent. In the HUD style, **HOLD TO TALK** is on the home screen too. See [Styles & Themes](styles-and-themes.md).

## What stays on the phone

* **Your speech is turned into words on the phone.** Your voice is never sent to Apple or Google to be recognised. If your phone can't do this for your language, the app says so, and you can type instead.
* **Only the words go to Auto**, like a typed message. They go to your workspace's AI providers, as your typed messages do. See [Privacy & AI](privacy-and-ai.md).
* **Auto's reply is spoken by a voice on the phone.**

## The microphone

* The app asks for the microphone and speech recognition the first time you press **HOLD TO TALK**, never before.
* The microphone opens only while you press. **MIC LIVE** shows whenever it is open.
* The screen stays on while you use voice. The app never listens in the background.
* A phone call, a change of headphones or leaving the app stops listening. Nothing is sent, and the microphone doesn't turn itself back on.

## Auto tells you it is an AI

When you start a voice session, Auto tells you once, on screen and out loud, that it is an AI. It doesn't repeat this during the session. The small "Auto is an AI" line stays on the voice screen too.

## Who can use voice

Owners, admins and editors. Viewers can't, because voice sends messages. They don't see the voice button, and on the HUD home **HOLD TO TALK** is greyed out with "Ask your workspace owner for editor access to talk to Auto".

## Voice on the phone and on the web

Phone voice uses your phone's own speech recognition and voice. Voice settings in Studio don't change it. See [Voice Chat](../chat/voice.md) for voice in Studio.

**Auto Live**, a live spoken conversation with Auto, comes after this, where your workspace can use it.
