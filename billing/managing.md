# Managing your plan

Everything happens in **Settings → Billing**, inside Studio; you never leave for another site. You need to be the workspace's owner or an admin.

## Buying a plan

1. Open **Settings → Billing** and choose a plan.
2. The payment form opens in the page. Enter your card and your billing address.
3. Buying for a business in the EU or UK? Add your **VAT number**. VAT is added or reverse-charged as it should be, and the total shown includes it.
4. Choose **Pay**. If your bank asks you to confirm the payment, its check appears on the page or in your banking app.
5. Your plan and your monthly credits are ready within a few seconds.

Card details go straight to our payment processor, Stripe; Automatos never sees or stores your card number.

## Changing your plan

* **Moving up.** The Billing tab shows what you'll pay now. It covers only the rest of the current month. Your new plan and its extra credits start straight away.
* **Moving down.** Nothing is charged. Your plan changes at the end of the month you've paid for. Changed your mind? Choose **Keep my plan**.
* **Business** is by invite. Once Automatos has offered it to your workspace, it appears on your Billing tab for 14 days, and you buy it the same way.

## Topping up credits

On Pro and Business, choose **Top up** on the Billing tab. The extra credits arrive as soon as the payment goes through, and they last 12 months.

## Your card and invoices

* **Change card:** enter the new card in the Billing tab. It's used for every payment from then on.
* **Invoices:** every invoice is listed on the Billing tab, and each one downloads as a PDF.

## If a payment doesn't go through

You get a notification, and the Billing tab shows that the payment failed. The payment is retried automatically over the next few days; update your card to fix it sooner. If every retry fails, the plan ends and your workspace moves to Own keys. Your workspace and anything on your own keys keep working.

## Cancelling

Choose **Cancel plan**. You keep your plan until the end of the month you've paid for; then the workspace moves to Own keys. Until then, **Resume plan** undoes it.

## Refunds

Contact Automatos. If a payment is refunded, the credits it bought are taken back.
